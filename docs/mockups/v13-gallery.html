  • Budget: every line has a Daily switch (counts toward daily spend). A new Office days (RTO) card sets the usual days, the peso per office day and the spontaneous-day count, and lets you tap days in the cutoff. RTO days and RTO contingency are budget lines.
  • Now: an In office today switch adds the office-day amount to today's safe-to-spend and draws it from the contingency.
  • Salary and tax: the Payslip tab is now a payslip builder: one payslip per cutoff (1st: income tax; 2nd: contributions and allowances). Move any line between cutoffs, override an amount, add your own lines, change the base split. The chart and year table show both cutoffs.
  • Wizards: contribution overrides moved into the builder; inflation is gone everywhere.
